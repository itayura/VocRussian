Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

Privyetik ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, store, and process when you use the Privyetik web and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide sync features, progress tracking, and AI tutoring services, we collect and store the following information:

  • Account Information: If you sign up, we store your email address and authentication credentials securely.
  • Study Progress: We store your vocabulary cards, review intervals (Leitner boxes), XP, streaks, attempts, and grammar quiz logs.
  • App Interactions & Feedback: We store user feedback reports and diagnostic logs.
  • AI Usage and IP Addresses: When you use AI-powered features (like the Grammar Sandbox or AI Tutor), we log the requested action along with your IP address to prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, and secure our API services.

2. Data Storage & Hosting

All sync data, including user progress, credentials, and logs, is securely stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Additionally, the application caches data locally in your browser storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage) to support offline access and fast load times.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with third-party service providers necessary to run the app:

  • Supabase: For authentication, database storage, and hosting database functions.
  • AI Service Providers (e.g. OpenAI): Your sentences and questions submitted to the grammar tutor are sent to LLM APIs to generate learning assistance. No personal identifiers are linked to these API requests.

4. Data Deletion & Account Deletion Requests

You have control over your data. In compliance with Google Play Developer policies, we provide a clear and straightforward process to delete your account and all associated data:

  • What you can delete in the app: You can delete individual vocabulary cards, clear custom lists, or completely reset your study history and Leitner boxes in the Settings panel of the Privyetik app.
  • Requesting complete account deletion: If you wish to delete your account permanently, you can request it by opening a ticket on our public GitHub Repository. We will process and complete your request within 48 hours.
  • What data is deleted: Upon account deletion, we permanently remove your email address, login credentials, custom vocabulary lists, Leitner box progress, XP points, streaks, and grammar quiz records from our Supabase database.
  • Data retention: We do not retain any of your personal data or study history after deletion. All records are purged immediately and permanently.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, feel free to open a ticket or contact us via our GitHub Repository: github.com/itayura/VocRussian.
